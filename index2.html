Catholic Identity

A Legacy of Faith,
Discipline & Excellence

Sacred Heart College Eziukwu Aba (SAHARCO) is a prestigious Catholic boys' secondary school founded in 1957 by the Catholic Diocese of Aba. We form young men of character, competence, and compassion — equipped to lead in every sphere of life.

Our mission is rooted in the Sacred Heart of Jesus: to bear witness to truth through academic rigor, spiritual formation, and service to humanity.

From the Principal

Leadership Rooted
in Faith & Purpose

"We are not merely forming students for examinations — we are forming young men whose lives will be an enduring testimony to truth, integrity, and service."

At Sacred Heart College Eziukwu Aba, we believe that education is the most powerful instrument of transformation — not merely intellectual, but moral, spiritual, and social. Our commitment is to every student who passes through these gates.

We hold fast to the values that have defined SAHARCO since 1957: academic excellence, Catholic faith, discipline, brotherhood, and service to God and nation. Under the patronage of the Sacred Heart and the guidance of the Catholic Diocese of Aba, our work is a vocation, not merely a profession.

A Word from Our
Bishop & Proprietor

"Education is not merely the transmission of knowledge — it is the formation of conscience, the lighting of a flame that no darkness can extinguish."

How to Apply

Admissions into JS1 and SS1 are conducted annually. Follow the steps below to begin your son's SAHARCO journey.

1
Obtain and complete the admission form from the school office or online portal.
2
Submit with birth certificate, Primary 6 certificate (JS1) or BECE results (SS1).
3
Sit the entrance examination on a designated date.
4
Upon offer of admission, complete acceptance and payment of fees.
